Provenance for every step a chip takes, from RTL to a booted board.

Each party that touches a part signs a small record of what it did. The records link by digest, and the buyer checks the whole chain before accepting parts or letting a device into its fleet.

  2. WaferMasks, fab, wafer sort
  3. Package/TestPackaging, final test
  4. AssemblyShipments, board build
  5. FirmwareBoot ROM, images, provisioning
